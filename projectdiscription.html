# PROJECT: DHANYA – Premium Rice & Wheat E-commerce Website Act as a senior UI/UX designer, frontend architect and full-stack developer. Build a complete, premium, production-quality e-commerce website for a fictional Indian grains business named **DHANYA – Pure Grains, Better Living**. This is a client demonstration project. It must look and behave like a real established grains brand, not a generic ecommerce template. ## 1. Business Background DHANYA is an Indian grains sourcing and selling business. The business: - Sources rice and wheat from carefully selected agricultural regions across India. - Focuses on grain quality, freshness, consistency and careful sourcing. - Offers different varieties of rice and wheat. - Serves households, retailers, restaurants, hotels and bulk buyers. - Accepts retail and wholesale enquiries. - Delivers orders across India. The brand should communicate trust, quality, transparency and a connection to Indian agriculture. Do not make unsupported medical, nutritional or pesticide-free claims. Product origin, sourcing and quality information should be presented as sample demonstration data unless verified. ## 2. Technology Stack Use: - React.js - Vite - JavaScript or TypeScript - Tailwind CSS or clean modular CSS - React Router DOM - Lucide React icons - Framer Motion for animations - Context API or Zustand for cart and global state - Recharts if analytics are needed Use reusable components and a clean project structure. ## 3. Design Direction Create a premium Indian agricultural brand aesthetic. Color palette: - Deep forest green: #183D2B - Olive green: #657C43 - Warm cream: #F8F5EB - Golden wheat: #D6A84F - Dark text: #25291F - White: #FFFFFF Design requirements: - Elegant typography. - Large, beautiful agricultural and food photography. - Clean product cards with subtle shadows. - Rounded corners used consistently. - Smooth hover effects. - Beautiful transitions and micro-interactions. - Premium whitespace and balanced layouts. - Modern editorial-style sections. Avoid generic Bootstrap-looking layouts and excessive gradients. The website must be fully responsive. ## 4. Website Pages Create these pages: 1. Home 2. Shop All 3. Rice Collection 4. Wheat Collection 5. Product Details 6. About Us 7. Our Sourcing 8. Bulk Orders 9. Contact Us 10. Cart 11. Checkout 12. Order Confirmation 13. FAQ ## 5. Homepage Design Build an impressive homepage with the following sections. ### Navigation - Brand logo: DHANYA. - Home. - Shop. - Rice. - Wheat. - Our Sourcing. - About. - Bulk Orders. - Contact. - Search icon. - Account icon if implemented. - Shopping cart with live item count. Add a responsive mobile navigation menu. ### Hero Section Create a visually striking full-width hero. Headline: "Good Grains. Honest Sourcing. Better Living." Supporting text: "Discover carefully selected rice and wheat, sourced from trusted agricultural regions and delivered to your doorstep." Buttons: - Explore Our Collection - Explore Our Sourcing Use beautiful close-up photography of rice, wheat and Indian agricultural landscapes. ### Featured Categories Display large image-based category cards: - Premium Rice - Everyday Rice - Basmati Rice - Traditional Rice - Premium Wheat - Everyday Wheat Each card should navigate to its corresponding category or filtered collection. ### Bestselling Products Show attractive product cards containing: - Product image. - Product name. - Variety. - Origin. - Weight. - Original price. - Discounted price. - Discount percentage. - Rating. - Add to Cart. - View Details. ### Why Choose DHANYA? Create four benefit cards: - Carefully Selected Grains. - Trusted Sourcing Regions. - Quality-Focused Selection. - Retail and Bulk Availability. Only make claims supported by actual product data. ### Our Sourcing Story Show an editorial section explaining how the business selects grains from different regions of India. Include an interactive India sourcing map or region cards with: - Region name. - Grain varieties. - Description. - Sample sourcing information. ### How It Works 1. Explore Products. 2. Choose Your Grain. 3. Place Your Order. 4. Receive Your Delivery. ### Bulk Orders Create a strong section targeting: - Hotels. - Restaurants. - Grocery Stores. - Distributors. - Wholesalers. - Food Businesses. CTA: Request a Bulk Quote. ### Testimonials Create visually attractive customer review cards. Use clearly identified sample/demo reviews rather than claiming fictional reviews are genuine verified customer feedback. ### FAQ Include questions about: - Product sourcing. - Available varieties. - Packaging sizes. - Delivery. - Bulk orders. - Returns and replacements. - Payment methods. ### Footer Include: - Brand introduction. - Quick links. - Product categories. - Contact details. - WhatsApp contact. - Instagram and Facebook links. - Newsletter subscription. - Privacy policy. - Terms and conditions. ## 6. Product Catalogue Create a realistic product catalogue with at least 24 sample products. Rice varieties: - Premium Basmati Rice. - Traditional Basmati Rice. - Sona Masoori Rice. - Kolam Rice. - Indrayani Rice. - Ambemohar Rice. - Jeera Rice. - Brown Rice. - Red Rice. - Gobindobhog Rice. - Steam Basmati Rice. - Everyday Non-Basmati Rice. Wheat varieties: - Sharbati Wheat. - Lokwan Wheat. - MP Wheat. - Maharashtra Wheat. - Whole Wheat. - Premium Milling Wheat. - Traditional Wheat. - Everyday Wheat. Provide different packaging sizes: - 1 kg. - 5 kg. - 10 kg. - 25 kg. - 50 kg where applicable. Each product must have: - Unique ID. - Product name. - Category. - Variety. - Origin region. - Description. - Product image. - Available weights. - Original price. - Discounted price. - Discount percentage. - Rating. - Sample review count. - Stock availability. - Product highlights. Use realistic INR pricing, but keep all prices editable from a centralized product data file. ## 7. Shop Functionality Implement working functionality: - Search products by name. - Filter by rice or wheat. - Filter by variety. - Filter by price. - Filter by origin. - Sort by price low to high. - Sort by price high to low. - Sort by popularity. - Sort by newest. - Grid and list view. - Pagination or load more. - Clear all filters. Category cards must navigate to the appropriate filtered catalogue. ## 8. Product Details Page When a user clicks a product, open a complete product details page. Include: - Large product image gallery. - Product name. - Description. - Origin information. - Variety. - Packaging size selector. - Original and discounted prices. - Quantity selector. - Availability. - Add to Cart. - Buy Now. - WhatsApp Enquiry. - Related products. The price should update when the customer selects a different package size. ## 9. Shopping Cart Implement a working shopping cart. Features: - Add products. - Remove products. - Change quantities. - Select packaging sizes. - Update subtotal. - Calculate delivery charges. - Calculate final total. - Empty cart state. - Continue shopping. - Proceed to checkout. Persist cart information using localStorage. ## 10. Checkout Create a professional checkout process. Customer details: - Full name. - Mobile number. - Email. - House number and street. - City. - State. - Pincode. - Landmark. Validate all fields. Show: - Order summary. - Product quantities. - Delivery fee. - Total amount. For the demo, provide a simulated online payment flow or a clearly marked integration-ready payment interface. Do not claim real payments have been processed. Add order confirmation after successful demo checkout. ## 11. Bulk Enquiry Create a dedicated bulk enquiry form. Fields: - Business name. - Contact person. - Mobile number. - Email. - Business type. - Rice or wheat requirement. - Product variety. - Required quantity in kg or quintals. - Delivery city. - Additional message. Allow the user to submit an enquiry through WhatsApp with all entered details formatted into a message. ## 12. WhatsApp Integration Add a floating WhatsApp button. Add WhatsApp enquiry buttons on: - Product details. - Bulk order section. - Contact page. Use a centralized WhatsApp number configuration. Use the wa.me URL with URL-encoded messages. Do not invent a real business phone number. Use an easily replaceable demo configuration. ## 13. Additional Features - Newsletter form with frontend validation. - Product wishlist. - Recently viewed products. - Toast notifications. - Loading skeletons. - Empty states. - Error states. - Back-to-top button. - Breadcrumb navigation. - Mobile-friendly sticky Add to Cart button. ## 14. SEO and Performance - Unique page titles. - Meta descriptions. - Semantic HTML. - Open Graph metadata. - Product structured data using JSON-LD with accurate demo information. - Optimized images. - Lazy loading. - Accessible buttons and forms. - Keyboard navigation. ## 15. Code Quality Create: - Reusable Header. - Footer. - ProductCard. - CategoryCard. - ProductFilters. - SearchBar. - CartDrawer. - QuantitySelector. - ReviewCard. - WhatsAppButton. - Newsletter. - Breadcrumbs. Organize files into components, pages, data, hooks, context/store, utilities and styles. Use centralized product data so products can easily be updated. Do not leave buttons non-functional or create dead links. ## 16. Final Requirements First inspect the existing project files and understand the current setup. Then implement the complete website, not just a design preview or a development plan. Do not delete existing working functionality unnecessarily. Do not stop after creating the homepage. Ensure every route works, all important interactions work, there are no console errors, and the website runs successfully. Run the project and verify the responsive layout at mobile, tablet and desktop widths. The final result should look like a premium Indian grains e-commerce brand that can be confidently demonstrated to a real business owner.
